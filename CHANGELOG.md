# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-09-28

First public release.

- **Library** (`engreact`, 11 modules): self-contained thermochemistry; kinetics and stoichiometry; analysis of
  rate data; ideal reactors with pressure drop; multiple reactions and reaction networks; energy balances with
  multiple steady states, transients and runaway; catalysis with internal and external transport and
  deactivation; residence-time distributions and nonideal-flow models; bioreactors; gas-solid reactions;
  bundled datasets.
- **Course**: 18 executed notebooks with learning objectives, "Inside the algorithm" sections and exercises,
  and 18 notebooks of worked solutions (54 exercises).
- **Validation**: 155 checks against analytic solutions, independent numerical integration, published
  thermochemical and textbook values, and recovery from noisy data.
