# Methods, data and references

SI units throughout (mol, m, s, J, K, Pa); rate constants in the units of the rate law; heats of reaction
per mole of the key reactant unless stated.

## Thermochemistry (`thermo`)

Standard enthalpies and Gibbs energies of formation from the NIST Chemistry WebBook; heat-capacity cubics
refitted in the style of Perry's Chemical Engineers' Handbook (Table 2-153), valid 298-1500 K and
extrapolated with caution beyond. Heats of reaction by Kirchhoff's law; equilibrium constants from Delta G(T)
with the full Cp integration; the constant-Delta-H van 't Hoff form as an approximation. Adiabatic
temperatures by an energy balance with the species Cp(T), bracketed at the first root (Turns, *An
Introduction to Combustion*, for the flame-temperature checks).

## Kinetics and rate data (`kinetics`, `rate_data`)

Arrhenius (1889) and its least-squares fitting in ln k vs 1/T with standard errors; stoichiometric tables
and the expansion factor as in Fogler, *Elements of Chemical Reaction Engineering* (6th ed.), Ch. 4; the
integral, differential (Fogler Ch. 7), half-life and initial-rate methods; nonlinear regression of the
integrated rate law with the Gauss-Newton covariance and the Akaike information criterion.

## Ideal reactors (`ideal`, `multiple`)

Design equations from the general mole balance (Fogler Ch. 1-2; Levenspiel, *Chemical Reaction
Engineering*, 3rd ed., Ch. 4-6); Levenspiel plots and reactors in series (Levenspiel Ch. 6); the recycle
reactor (Levenspiel Ch. 6); Ergun's equation (*Chem. Eng. Prog.* 48, 1952) and the packed-bed pressure-drop
equation in Fogler's form (Ch. 5); Bateman's solution of series first-order reactions (*Proc. Camb. Phil.
Soc.* 15, 1910); selectivity and yield definitions of Fogler Ch. 8; general networks integrated with LSODA.

## Energy balances (`energy`)

Adiabatic and heat-exchange PFRs (Fogler Ch. 11-12); the CSTR heat-generation and heat-removal curves, the
slope condition for stability and the ignition-extinction diagram (van Heerden 1953; Fogler Ch. 12;
Levenspiel Ch. 9); transient CSTR and batch reactors with jacket cooling; Semenov's criterion for thermal
explosion (Semenov 1928; Frank-Kamenetskii, *Diffusion and Heat Transfer in Chemical Kinetics*).

## Catalysis (`catalysis`)

Langmuir adsorption and LHHW rate laws (Hougen & Watson 1943; Fogler Ch. 10); Thiele modulus (1939) and the
effectiveness factors of Aris, *The Mathematical Theory of Diffusion and Reaction in Permeable Catalysts*;
the Weisz-Prater (1954) and Mears (1971) criteria; falsified kinetics (Weisz & Prater); Frossling's
correlation; deactivation laws and the temperature-time trajectory of Fogler Ch. 10.

## Residence-time distributions (`rtd`)

Danckwerts (*Chem. Eng. Sci.* 2, 1953) for E(t), F(t) and the closed-vessel dispersion conversion; the
tanks-in-series model and the variance relations of Levenspiel Ch. 13-14; the closed-closed dispersion model
solved by the method of lines with Danckwerts boundary conditions and validated against Levenspiel's variance
formula; the segregation model and Zwietering's maximum mixedness (*Chem. Eng. Sci.* 11, 1959).

## Bioreactors and gas-solid reactions (`bio`, `heterogeneous`)

Michaelis-Menten (1913) with the inhibition classes and the linearised plots of Lineweaver & Burk and Hanes;
Monod (1949); chemostat theory (Herbert, Elsworth & Telling 1956; Shuler & Kargi, *Bioprocess Engineering*);
the shrinking-core model of Yagi & Kunii and Levenspiel Ch. 25.

## Data (`datasets`)

The Arrhenius-parameter table follows Laidler, *Chemical Kinetics* (3rd ed.) and Atkins' *Physical Chemistry*
(indicative textbook values). All pseudo-experimental files are generated by `tools/make_course_data.py`
from the library's own models with the parameters, noise and deliberate faults stated in their headers; the
N2O5 rate constant is of the order of Daniels & Johnston's 1921 value, the saponification constant of the
literature range.

## General references

Fogler, *Elements of Chemical Reaction Engineering*, 6th ed. (2020) · Levenspiel, *Chemical Reaction
Engineering*, 3rd ed. (1999) · Froment, Bischoff & De Wilde, *Chemical Reactor Analysis and Design*, 3rd ed.
(2011) · Hill & Root, *Introduction to Chemical Engineering Kinetics and Reactor Design*, 2nd ed. (2014) ·
Rawlings & Ekerdt, *Chemical Reactor Analysis and Design Fundamentals*, 2nd ed. (2012) · Shuler & Kargi,
*Bioprocess Engineering*, 2nd ed. (2002).
